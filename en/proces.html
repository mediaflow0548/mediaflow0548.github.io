How it works

From first contact to final result — in five clear steps.

Contact & quote

You get in touch via email, phone or social media — I usually respond within a few hours. We briefly discuss your wishes and I'll send you a free quote.

Planning

If we agree, we'll schedule a filming day that suits you. I take weather, light and your opening hours into account.

Filming

I come to your location and film everything with drone and camera. This usually takes a few hours — depending on the size of the project.

Editing

I edit the footage into a cinematic video with color grading and music. This phase requires the most time and attention.

Delivery

Within about 2 weeks you'll receive the final result via a link that preserves the quality perfectly. Want something adjusted? I'll keep adapting until it's exactly right. For very large projects, this can take a little longer.

What I need from you

Not much — but this helps me get the best result.

📍 Location & access

A clear address and permission to film (for real estate: approval of the owner).

💭 Your wishes

What will you use the video for? Website, social media, brochures? That determines the style of the edit.

🌤️ Flexibility

Drone work depends on the weather. Sometimes a filming day needs to be rescheduled — at no extra cost.
