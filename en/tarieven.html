What affects the price?

Three factors determine the final price of your project.

🚗 Travel distance

For longer distances, I charge travel costs. I live in Holten — locations in the surrounding area (Twente/Salland) are the most advantageous.

⏱️ Delivery time

The delivery time is about 2 weeks — I aim to finish and send your video within that time. Rush delivery is possible at a surcharge. Occasionally, for example with a very large project, it may take slightly longer.

📦 Scope

More shots, a longer video or additional services means a higher price — but also more value per assignment.
